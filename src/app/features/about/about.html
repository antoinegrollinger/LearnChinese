汉字

About Hanzi Workshop

Learn Chinese characters by understanding how they are built: the part that gives their meaning, the part that gives their sound, and the order of their strokes.

Made by Antoine Grollinger. Questions, ideas and feedback are welcome at {{ email }}.

What you can do

  • 字
    Study characters

    Stroke order, writing practice, meaning and sound components.

  • 词
    Collect words

    Built from your characters, with meanings from CC-CEDICT.

  • ✍
    Review

    Write them, or give their pinyin, until each one is right.

  • 📈
    Follow your progress

    Past reviews, hardest characters, and labels to group them.

  • 👥
    Learn with others

    Friends, communities, and reviews you choose to share.

Thanks to Hanzi Writer, Make Me a Hanzi, CC-CEDICT (CC BY-SA 4.0), hanzi and Complete HSK Vocabulary. Hanzi Workshop is open source, under the Apache 2.0 license.
